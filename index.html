Methodology & Data Notes

  • Source: Tax Foundation Europe, Carbon Taxes in Europe, 2026. Accessed 2026.
  • Carbon Tax Rate: Rate per tonne of CO₂-equivalent (€ and US$) as of 2026. Euro is the primary currency for all comparisons; USD is informational only.
  • GHG Coverage: Percentage of the jurisdiction's total greenhouse gas emissions subject to the carbon tax, as of 2025.
  • Adjusted C-efficiency Ratio (2023): Measures how efficiently the carbon tax raises revenue relative to the theoretical maximum from taxing all GHG emissions at the full rate. A published value of 0 may reflect no data available for 2023, not necessarily zero efficiency.
  • Other Carbon Price: "Complementary" = another pricing instrument (e.g. EU ETS) covers separate emissions sectors in parallel. "Supplementary" = it fills coverage gaps left by the carbon tax.
  • EU ETS is shown as a reference benchmark only and is excluded from all KPI and chart calculations.
  • KPI averages are computed only over the jurisdictions currently visible after filters. Averages (Avg Tax Rate, Avg GHG Coverage) are not shown when fewer than 2 jurisdictions are selected, as a single-jurisdiction average has no comparative meaning.
  • Scatter plot shows association between tax rate and coverage — not causation. High tax rates do not necessarily reduce emissions or indicate more effective policy.
  • C-efficiency chart only plots jurisdictions where ratio > 0 (18 of 24 have a non-zero published value for 2023; the remaining 6 have no published 2023 figure available).
